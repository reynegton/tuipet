# EGG AUDIT — 2026-07-25

The method: run the system, don't read it.  Five probe batteries drove the
whole egg surface — the bank (`eggs.json.gz`), the full hatch lifecycle for
all 46 eggs, the unlock table over staged progress profiles, all three egg
screens with geometry measurement, the four historical bank migrations, and
the generational commit through the REAL app handlers.  Three defects, one
shared root for two of them.  Shipped v0.5.262; pins in
`tests/test_egg_audit.py` (all six fix-pins verified to FAIL on the old
code).

## E1 — Towns sold the never-ownable eggs  ✅ FIXED

`shop._sellable_eggs` excluded only the five starters, so the five
`can_perm: FALSE` lineage eggs — Kuramon, Puttimon, Fufumon, Ryuda Egg,
Lalamon Egg ("Direct descendant of…", "Carries the X-Antibody",
"obedience 120+") — reached town shelves (towns 0/2/5 in the current
rotation) and `town_egg_buy` pushed them into `persistence.egg_own`:
permanent ownership of eggs whose whole design is *this generation only*.
`eggmigrate._sane_owned` exists to strip exactly these from `eggs_owned`
"however they snuck in" — the shop was selling, for 800 bits, what the
repair pass deletes.

**Fix**: the shelf filter skips `can_perm: FALSE` rows, and the single buy
path (`town_egg_buy`, the one door both the shop's Eggs tab and the
unrouted TownEggPanel go through) refuses them outright, softly:
"A lineage egg — it hatches for those who earn it."

## E2 — An egg re-roll pumped the generation  ✅ FIXED

Options→new egg on an unhatched egg "hands off without ceremony" (the
skipped ceremony is the *confirm*), but `_hatch_new` treated the hand-off
as a full generational commit with `gen = generation + 1`.  Measured: five
re-rolls took a gen-2 egg to gen 7, and the eventual hatch recorded
`max_gen 7` — opening every gen-gated egg and the shop's gen-5
`egg_of_destiny` with no lives lived.  A generation is a LIFE:
`snapshot_prev_gen` has always refused to record an egg; the commit now
agrees.

**Fix**: `_hatch_new` detects an unhatched, undead outgoing egg and keeps
`gen = self.pet.generation` — a re-pick, not a succession.

## E3 — The re-roll destroyed the inheritance  ✅ FIXED

The same commit ran `bank_bonus_seed(final_care_grade())` on the EGG
(a flat 2), stomping the dead elder's banked seed (measured: 9 → 2), and
the etched Digimemory — already taken from the bank by the discarded
shell's `_grant_digimemory` — died with the shell (measured: payload and
chip gone, bank empty).  Bought goods and the wallet died the same way
(the home shop opens for an egg).

**Fix**: on a re-pick the whole estate moves to the new shell — bits, bag,
trophies, `trophies_won`, DNA bank, the taken `evol_bonus` seed and the
etched memory, one chip exactly (the payload-without-chip guard from the
digimemory audit is honored).

## E1's ripple — the band collapse (caught by yesterday's shops pin)

Cutting the 5 lineage eggs left a 36-egg pool, and the town rotation
stepped by the band width (6) — which divides 36, so all 26 towns
collapsed into 6 identical bands, breaking "all shops feel unique"
(`test_no_two_towns_sell_an_identical_egg_band`).  **Fix**: the rotation
stride bumps until co-prime with the pool; 26 distinct bands measured.
This is why the ratchets run after every fix — the pin did its job.
