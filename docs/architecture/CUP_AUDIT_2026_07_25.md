# CUP AUDIT — the tournament, end to end (2026-07-25)

Joel: "lets do a full blown cup audit next."

Scope: the cadence (24 hourly slots, the featured cup, seasons, holidays),
eligibility and the stake, the bracket and its NPC field, the in-bracket
ramp, the payout ladder, the trophy/egg gates, and every page the panel
can draw.  Method, as with the item, battle and adventure sweeps: **run
the cup** — hundreds of brackets, a year of calendar days, and the real
app driven to a title.

**Verdict: the cup's MACHINE is sound; its SCREEN was four rows too tall.**
Everything the tournament said to you — who you were about to fight, who
just advanced, how to start the match — was being drawn below the bottom
of the LCD.

---

## 1. F1 — THE SHOW PAGES OVERFLOWED THE BOX  ✅ FIXED

Each cup page composed itself as:

```
menu.bar(...)      1 row     the title bar
scene              12 rows   the FULL-height arena (FIGHT_ROWS)
"vs <foe> ★N"      1 row
menu.note(...)     1 row
menu.footer(...)   1 row
                 = 16 rows into a 12-row LCD
```

Measured **in the running app** (the widget's own content region, not a
string count): `#lcd` is 12 rows, the panel handed it 16, and the bottom
four were dropped on the floor.  What the player never saw:

| page | lost to the clip |
|------|------------------|
| faceoff | the arena's floor row · `vs Gorimon[Da]  your ★0` · the cup line · **`SPACE fight  ESC leave`** |
| introductions | the floor row · the vs line · `Gorimon [Da] enters!` · `— introductions —` |
| advancing field | the floor row · `SandYanmamon advances` · `Yanmamon / FlameWizarmon advance too.` · `— the next round forms —` |
| ceremony | the floor row · the trophy stars · `The trophy is yours!` · `— the crowd roars —` |
| select | its key footer (a word-for-word copy of the strip) |

Two consequences beyond the missing words: the arena lost its own **floor
row**, where every sprite baselines — and because the show pages pushed
their scene down one row for the title bar while the fight's scene starts
at row 0, **the fighters jumped a row at the bell**, which is exactly the
seam v0.5.240 set out to make seamless.

**The fix is the family law**, the one the raid page got in v0.5.183 and
the battle panel has always had: *the LCD is pure scene.*  The show pages
are now 12 rows of arena and nothing else, and the words moved to the
surfaces that were always visible:

- **the strip narrates** — "Seadramon [Da] enters!", "Dorulumon advances",
  "★ the trophy is yours! ★" (verified live);
- **the card carries the foe** — `vs Vegimon[Vi]` sits above the trophy
  count, where a fight's context lives everywhere else in the game;
- **the select page dropped its footer**, the FOOTER PURGE the QOL sweep
  ran across sound/options/themes/shop and this page missed.

## 2. HELD UNDER LOAD (measured, not read)

- **Bracket integrity, 200 cups**: always 8 entrants, YOU in it exactly
  once, exactly three rounds to a title, every opponent a real entrant,
  and the field advancing without ever losing or duplicating you.
- **The stake and the ladder**: the fee leaves the purse at entry; a
  quarterfinal exit pays 0 (the stake is eaten), a semi exit a third of
  the purse, a final loss a half, the title the whole thing — the
  bit-sink design intact.  A pet that DIES mid-bracket is not crowned.
- **The economy is bounded by the clock, not by the payout.**  Net per cup:
  fresh Champion +159, trained +341, veteran with a mega lock +1,483 (53
  titles in 60 cups).  That last number is large, but cups are hourly —
  one slot per real hour plus the daily featured — so the road still
  out-earns them per minute of play.  Recorded, not changed: the "champion
  nets +75% of the purse" ladder is the shipped design.
- **The calendar**: 211 distinct featured cups across a year, never a day
  without one, all four festivals landing once, and all 24 hourly slots
  resolving to real trophies.
- **The egg gates line up.**  The guide says "Win Summer Open #147" and the
  gate keys on trophy **146** — the label is 1-based on purpose ("#0 reads
  like a bug"), so they are the same cup.  I chased this as a suspected
  off-by-one and it was clean; now pinned, because a real off-by-one here
  would send a tamer to win the wrong tournament forever.
